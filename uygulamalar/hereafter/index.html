Hereafter: Letters & Legacy

Final letters, will & wishes

Download on the App Store

Some things are left unsaid. Hereafter gives you a private, secure place to finally say them — so when you're gone, the people you love can hear your voice, read your words, and know what you wished for them.

What you can do

  • Write letters — To each person who matters. Personal, private, and waiting for them.
  • Leave voice messages — Record the words that writing can't capture. Add videos too.
  • Settle your debts — Record what you owe and what's owed to you. Leave with peace.
  • Share your wishes — How do you want to be remembered? Who gets what?
  • Store vital information — Documents, insurance, accounts. Make it easy for them.
  • Create a Legacy File — Everything in one PDF. Print it, seal it, or share it securely.

Completely prıvate

Nothing leaves your phone. No servers, no accounts, no internet required. Lock the app with Face ID or fingerprint whenever you like.

This app isn't about death. It's about love — finishing what you started, saying what matters, and leaving the people you care about with something real.

Your words. Your trace. Theirs to keep.

Note: Hereafter is not a legal will. For official documents, consult a notary.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Nothing leaves your phone: no servers, no accounts and no internet required.

What can I leave behind?

Letters to each person, voice and video messages, your wishes, debts and vital information, all collected into a Legacy File PDF.

Can I lock the app?

Yes, with Face ID or your fingerprint.
