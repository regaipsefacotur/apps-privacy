Tally: Credit Ledger & Debts

Customer tabs for small shops

Download on the App Store

Tally is the digital credit ledger for small merchants — grocers, bakers, butchers, corner shops. Track what customers owe, record payments, and send a polite reminder via WhatsApp, all from your pocket.

Features

  • Add debts and payments in seconds — name, amount, done.
  • Real-time balance per customer, automatically calculated.
  • WhatsApp reminder — send a payment nudge with their balance in one tap.
  • Share account statements — transparent history with any customer.
  • App lock — Face ID / fingerprint keeps your ledger private.
  • CSV export — your full ledger in a spreadsheet.
  • Backup & restore — JSON backup, restore on any phone.

Your data stays wıth you

No internet required, no account, no cloud. All records live only on your device. Your ledger is yours alone.

Put the paper ledger away — your book is now in your pocket.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Who is Tally for?

Small merchants such as grocers, bakers, butchers and corner shops who keep a customer credit ledger.

Can I remind customers to pay?

Yes. Send a WhatsApp reminder with their balance in one tap.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. No internet, account or cloud is needed; you can back up and restore with a JSON file.
