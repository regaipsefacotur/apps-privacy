CallShield — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Data Collection

CallShield does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All blocking rules, preferences, and phone number prefixes you configure are stored exclusively on your device using Apple's App Groups framework.

How It Works

CallShield uses Apple's CallKit Call Directory Extension to block or identify unwanted calls. The phone number prefixes you add are passed directly to iOS on your device. No data ever leaves your device or is sent to any server.

No Analytics

CallShield does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks. There are no third-party SDKs integrated into the app.

No Network Requests

CallShield makes zero network requests. The app works entirely offline.
